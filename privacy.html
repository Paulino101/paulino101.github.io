Card Tracker privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026.

Card Tracker is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, to keep the records of a small trading card resale business.

What it accesses

  • Google Sheets: the owner's own spreadsheet of sales, stock and expenses, which it reads and updates.
  • Gmail: it reads the owner's order, payout and receipt emails to record them in that spreadsheet, and sends the owner a daily summary email to the owner's own address.

What it does with that data

  • The data is used only to keep the owner's business records.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with or sent to anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising.
  • Card Tracker's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
